OB Recorder v0.1

EVALUATIVE INSTRUMENT — instructor / examiner use only. Architecturally separate from The Left Seat companion and the Digital Hangar (developmental / SBT track). Never present to trainees as a self-assessment or mentoring tool.
Built on the EASA ORCA cycle — Observe · Record · Classify · Assess. Tapping an OB performs Record and Classify in one action; the instrument then counts evidence on the three grading dimensions — how many distinct OBs, how often demonstrated, how well (effect on safety of the TEM outcome) — and assembles a debrief pack. Assess stays with the instructor: the instrument never assigns a grade.
OB catalogue: ICAO nine-competency framework (PANS-TRG / Doc 9995 lineage; IATA CAE for Pilots, Instructors & Evaluators GM) — verify wording and numbering against the operator's OM-D before formal use · workflow per the EASA ORCA methodology (Observe · Record · Classify · Assess) · grading dimensions per the IATA/EASA competency assessment guidance (OB count, frequency, effect on safety of the TEM outcome) · all data stays on this device (localStorage); use trainee codes, not names · EVAL-track artifact: not part of, and not to be cross-referenced from, the developmental companion ecosystem · HFHorizons / TUC · Pechlivanis K.
